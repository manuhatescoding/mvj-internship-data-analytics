## Project 2: Bengaluru Rainfall Forecasting

**Objective:** Analyse historical Bengaluru rainfall and compare forecasting methods.

**Dataset:** Bengaluru Monthly Rainfall Data.

**Techniques used:**
- Time-series preparation
- Historical rainfall visualisation
- 3-month moving average
- 12-month moving average
- Simple Exponential Smoothing
- MAE and RMSE evaluation

## Limitations

- Clustering results depend on the selected features and number of clusters.
- Rainfall forecasts are estimates, not guarantees.
- Simple exponential smoothing does not explicitly capture seasonality.
