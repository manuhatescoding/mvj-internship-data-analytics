 Namma Metro KPI Tree

Objective
Understand passenger demand, revenue indicators, and service performance to identify opportunities to improve Namma Metro operations.

 1. Ridership
- Total ridership: Total passenger journeys during a selected period.
- Daily ridership: Passenger journeys per day.
- Peak-hour passenger share: Peak-hour passenger journeys / Total passenger journeys × 100.
- Ridership growth: (Current-period ridership - Previous-period ridership) / Previous-period ridership × 100.

 2. Revenue Proxies
- Estimated fare revenue: Sum of passenger fares collected, where data is available.
- Average fare per trip: Estimated fare revenue / Number of passenger journeys.
- Revenue per passenger: Estimated revenue / Total passengers.
- Important limitation: Revenue estimates depend on available fare and ridership data and may not equal audited financial revenue.

 3. Service Performance
- Train frequency: Number of train services operating per hour.
- Service availability: Actual service hours / Planned service hours × 100.
- Peak-hour demand: Passenger journeys during defined peak hours.
- Crowding indicator: Passenger demand compared with available capacity, if capacity data is available.

 Types of Analytics
- Descriptive: Measure historical ridership and service performance.
- Diagnostic: Investigate reasons for changes in ridership or crowding.
- Predictive: Forecast future ridership using historical data.
- Prescriptive: Recommend service-frequency or capacity adjustments based on demand.

 Data Requirements
Potential data includes daily ridership, hourly passenger counts, fare information, train schedules, service disruptions, and passenger capacity.

 Limitations
This KPI tree defines proposed metrics. Actual performance values and operational conclusions require reliable data. Metric definitions, time periods, and data sources should be documented before calculation.
